RIVERSIDE LEARNING COLLECTIVE — COURSE GUIDE
Academic year: 2026–2027

ASSESSMENT
The course grade is made up of weekly practice (20%), a field report (35%), a final project (35%), and participation (10%). The final project is evaluated on evidence quality, clear reasoning, and a useful reflection. A student needs 60% overall to complete the course.

DEADLINES
Assignments are due at 5:00 PM local time on the date shown in the course calendar. The course calendar lists the field report deadline as 14 March 2027. Work submitted within 24 hours after a deadline loses 10 percentage points. Later work requires an extension approved before the deadline. The field report deadline was changed in an addendum to 16 March 2027; the course calendar has not yet been updated. When dates disagree, follow the dated addendum and ask the course office to confirm.

ATTENDANCE AND SUPPORT
Students should attend at least 8 of the 10 weekly seminars. If illness or access needs prevent attendance, email the course office; an equivalent reflection can replace a missed seminar. Peer tutoring is available on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 3:00 to 5:00 PM in Room 204 and online. The instructor holds office hours on Wednesday from 1:00 to 2:30 PM by appointment.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
Students may use AI tools to brainstorm or check explanations, but submitted claims and citations must be reviewed by the student. AI-generated text must be disclosed in the assignment note. Never submit private data or another student's work to an AI service. A suspected citation error should be corrected before submission; ask the instructor when source wording is ambiguous.

ACCESS
Materials are available as accessible HTML and tagged PDF. Students may request large-print copies, extra time, or an alternate presentation format without explaining a diagnosis. Contact access@riverside.example at least five working days before a scheduled assessment.
